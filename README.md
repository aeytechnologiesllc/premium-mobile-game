# Aelora — Whispers of the Enchanted Forest

A premium 2.5D mobile runner-adventure game.

## About

Play as Aelora, a young woman who follows a mysterious melody into an enchanted forest. Run, slide, jump, and use magic through five beautiful hand-painted worlds. Collect pieces of the Forest Queen's shattered crown and decide the fate of the magical realm.

## Tech Stack

- **Engine:** Unity 6.4
- **Animation:** Spine (2D skeletal animation)
- **Art:** Midjourney (AI-generated backgrounds and assets)
- **Platforms:** iOS & Android

## Game Features

- Swipe-based controls (jump, slide, dodge, magic)
- 5 unique worlds with parallax-scrolling painted backgrounds
- Spine-animated character with flowing dress and hair physics
- Outfit customization system
- Narrative fairy tale storyline with choice-based ending

## Worlds

1. Moonlit Garden
2. Cherry Blossom Woods
3. Crystal Waterfall Caves
4. Golden Autumn Forest
5. Starlight Canopy
